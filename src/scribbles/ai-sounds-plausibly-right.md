Was reading a mailing-list on OpenBSD, found a lesson for me on how to be careful when AI
made an explanation that sounds plausibly right. The discussion went from technical to
personal. It was due to a person who's less knowledgeable arguing with AI explanations to
someone who's an expert. Also even the proposed solution could be work on other side, it
doesn't mean it is acceptable since the maintainer knows more how it impacts the system.

I guess I should reflect this when contributing to a project, always be mindful about
how it will fit in the system and learn from a human expert. Not from confident speculation
algorithm.
